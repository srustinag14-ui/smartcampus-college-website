Admission procedure

  1. Register online. Create an applicant account with your email and mobile number.
  2. Fill the application. Choose up to three courses in order of preference.
  3. Upload documents. Marks cards, entrance rank card, ID proof and a recent photograph.
  4. Pay the application fee. You will receive a receipt by email.
  5. Attend counselling. Verify originals and confirm your seat with the first-year fee.
